A Full Day

The Curator's Day.

A recommended arc through the property and the coastline — from sunrise on the terrace to a final glass in the cellar vault. Bookable as a complete day, or pick individual elements à la carte.

07:00
Sunrise on your private terrace

Coffee and warm sfogliatelle served at first light, with a hand-annotated weather and tide card for the day ahead.

09:30
Lemon grove walk with our head gardener

Two hours through the terraced Sfusato groves above the property, ending with a private limoncello press.

13:00
Al-fresco lunch at the Olive Pergola

Four courses of coastal terroir — hand-rolled sourdough, buffalo mozzarella, line-caught fish, wild honeycomb.

15:30
Riva yacht departure from the private jetty

A four-hour coastal expedition past Positano and the Li Galli archipelago, with a swim stop in a hidden cove.

20:30
Cliffside Celestial Dinner

Seven courses on the open-air cliff deck, guided by our resident astronomer and his Swarovski telescope.

23:00
A final glass in the Cellar Vault

Nightcap of rare Barolo or a coastal amaro, poured by the sommelier on duty. Cigar lounge open until late.
