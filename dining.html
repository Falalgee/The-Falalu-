Culinary Direction

Where the sea meets the soil.

Led by Chef Matteo Vianello and culinary director Hélène Vasseur, our dining philosophy centers on hyper-local coastal ingredients and slow, intentional preparation.

Line-caught wild turbot, heirloom herbs cultivated on our southern terraces, and aged balsamic from Emilia. The garden reserve spans 4.2 biodynamic hectares overlooking the bay — every plate begins somewhere within sight.
